Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

LeafReady is a Canadian citizenship test preparation app. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

LeafReady does not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not track your identity, and does not transmit any data to external servers.

Data Stored on Your Device

Quiz statistics (scores, number of questions answered, streaks) are stored locally on your device using standard system storage (AsyncStorage on mobile). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Third-Party Services

LeafReady does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

LeafReady does not knowingly collect any data from users of any age, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
