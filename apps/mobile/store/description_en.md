## Full description

Prepare for your Canadian citizenship test with LeafReady — the most complete practice app based exclusively on the official "Discover Canada" guide.

**450 questions. All categories. Both official languages.**

Whether you're cramming the night before or studying over several weeks, LeafReady adapts to your pace with flexible quiz modes and detailed progress tracking.

### Features

- **450 practice questions** covering all topics from the official guide
- **Timed mode** — simulate real test conditions (20 questions, 30 minutes)
- **Practice mode** — study at your own pace, no time pressure
- **Category filter** — focus on your weak areas (history, government, rights, symbols, and more)
- **Detailed results** — review every answer after each quiz
- **Progress tracking** — monitor your improvement over time
- **Bilingual** — full English and French support
- **Works offline** — no internet connection required
- **Free** — no ads, no subscription, no paywalls

### Topics covered

Rights & Responsibilities · Who We Are · Indigenous History · European History ·
Modern Canada · How Government Works · Federal Elections · Justice System ·
Canadian Symbols · Economy · Regions of Canada

### Built with care

LeafReady is an open-source project built for newcomers to Canada. All questions are
sourced directly from the official "Discover Canada" study guide published by
Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada.
